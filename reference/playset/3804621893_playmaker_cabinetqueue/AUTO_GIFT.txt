AUTO GIFT - DIRECT SCRIPTED TRANSFERS (EXPERIMENTAL)

Open Mod Menu > Theplaymaker > Diplomacy > Auto Gift, or right-click the top
diplomacy resource. Right-click again to close.
Add nations opens a multi-select picker: choose countries in the list or on the
map, then Confirm. Click a selected list row to deselect it; Clear resets the
selection, and Cancel discards it. Confirm adds the batch and returns to Auto Gift.
Set the gold reserve and turn Auto Gift ON. Enabling runs the first
check immediately. It checks again monthly or when Run now is clicked.
OFF is the default, including campaigns which used the retired slider automation.
The target list scrolls. Targets, ON/OFF, cooldowns and reserve save with the campaign.

Each eligible recipient gets the same gold deducted from your treasury, 10 favors
owed to you, and opinion_gift at scale 2 (normal modifier capped at +40 opinion).
No diplomat is spent and no gift/country window is opened.
Targets at war, undiscovered, outside diplomatic range, without land, too near the
favor cap, or too expensive to leave the reserve are skipped and retried later.

PRICE CALCULATION
2 * max(25, sender monthly_income_total + 2 * recipient monthly_income_total)
  * max(0.01, 1 + sender send_gift_cost_modifier), with a minimum of 1 gold.
monthly_income_total is the engine's scaled income value; it differs from gross
monthly_income_trade_and_tax and from the tax base displayed on country panels.
The quote is shown to two decimals. The transfer uses the full fixed-point value
without rounding up to whole gold. The same stored quote drives affordability,
the sender debit and the recipient credit.

EXECUTION
Transfers execute directly inside the script, synchronously and without menus.
There is no fire_generic_action, temporary reserve deduction, UI price binding or
native gift slider involved. The reserve is checked against the full cost before
sending. Cooldown is recorded before payment to prevent duplicate transfers.

COOLDOWNS
The tool records its own 120-month cooldown per recipient and checks the game's
native has_gifted_gold_to cooldown as well. Removing/re-adding a target, Run now,
or OFF/ON cannot bypass the custom cooldown. The normal Send Gift UI, country
shortcuts, recipient picker and map context actions now check the same custom
cooldown and display its reason. This applies to cooldowns already in your save.
The engine does not expose an effect to write its native gift-history timer; this
is a UI restriction using the saved timer, not a native history entry. Direct gifts
still do not fire the native gift-sent hook or satisfy gift-history mission checks.
Native AI/engine automation is not governed by this UI restriction.

FIRST IN-GAME TEST
1. Save, restart EU5, then add one target while Auto Gift remains OFF.
2. Note both treasuries, favors owed to you, and the displayed cost.
3. Enable: verify one transfer, exactly 10 favors owed to you, and Gift cooldown.
4. Run now twice and toggle OFF/ON: no second gift to the same target.
5. Remove/re-add that target; verify its cooldown remains. Save/reload and repeat.
6. Add multiple targets and set a high reserve; verify unaffordable gifts are skipped.
7. Test more than seven targets, manual-gift cooldown, and a target at war.
8. Switch OFF, advance a month: no transfers.

The previous GUI-driven gift worker remains retired. This version uses pmcqsg
state and never opens or operates SendGiftWindow.
