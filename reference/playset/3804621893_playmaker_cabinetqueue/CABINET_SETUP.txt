CABINET SETUP
Open Opening Setup, then Cabinet Setup.
Select one of the six numbered positions, then choose an action below.
Choosing an existing favorite swaps its position. Reset defaults restores Values,
queued Control, queued Noblesse Oblige, Auto Integrate, Auto Assimilate, and
Diplomatic Endeavors. X closes the menu; changes are applied immediately.
Preferences are stored on the player country and persist when the campaign is saved.
Changing favorites does not change any currently assigned cabinet action.
Locked special actions are hidden until their normal unlock conditions are met.
The native cabinet picker still validates assignment and asks for targets when needed.
Normal Control/Noblesse/Integration/Assimilation duplicates are omitted from choices.

Maintenance: CABINET_FAVORITES.json records stable IDs used in existing saves.
Do not renumber these IDs when adding future actions.
