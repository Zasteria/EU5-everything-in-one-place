AUTO ASSIMILATE AREA TO CORE

Assign the new Auto Assimilate Area to Core cabinet action after unlocking
the native Assimilate Area action. Only one cabinet slot can use it at a time.
It automatically promotes your primary culture in eligible provinces with integrated
non-core locations across an area, then selects another area once no eligible
provinces remain. It stops when no eligible area remains.

Uses the native area bonuses (0.02 assimilation speed and -0.2 migration
attraction), scaled by cabinet effective skill. It avoids areas with existing
culture-promotion assignments when choosing a new target, and shares province
reservations with Auto Assimilate to prevent overlapping assignments.
Cored locations within active provinces receive the bonus too. Each province
is released once all its locations are cores, even if culture conversion is
incomplete. The action moves to another area when no eligible provinces remain. Bonuses persist until explicitly removed; daily
checks only replace them if missing or cabinet skill changes.

The daily worker refreshes eligible locations and cleans up on cancellation,
ownership changes, primary-culture changes, or loss of the native unlock.
Culture Convert All still assigns the existing province action; select the
area action separately in the cabinet.

Validation: structural checks passed. In-game verification is still required
after restarting EU5. The previous two-day modifiers upgrade on the next refresh
or when they expire; cancel and reassign the action to apply immediately.
