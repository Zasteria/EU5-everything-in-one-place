在1.2版本中，由于同一个宗主国的附庸共享同一个强制转变宗教/文化的cd，玩家在10年内只能强制转变一个附庸的文化/宗教。同时，在1.2版本中，系统会动态判定领土的核心状态，那些主流和接受文化人口<50%的地块会失去核心。这些新更新的内容使得玩家的扩张变为极为缓慢且毫无意义，只会让游戏在慢节奏中变得相当无聊。

为了解决以上问题，本mod做了如下改动：
1.将“整合”地区的效果改动为：最大控制力+15%，每月控制力+0.05%，本地分离主义+10%。取消了本地人口同化速度-10的debuff。这使得游戏中的整合地区十分接近核心地区。
2.将“强制附庸转变宗教”和“强制附庸转变文化”的cd改为3天。（在游戏中仍然显示10年，不过效果是的确生效的）
3.在罗马帝国dlc更新后，拉丁-罗马价值观的效果使得广大玩家极度不满意，我将他们的效果改变为如下效果：

拉丁化：
出售效率+2.50%
预期外交支出-20.00%
每月人文倾向+0.10
每月财阀政治倾向+0.10
允许拉丁文化运动运动

罗马派
稳定度投资花费-20.00%
正统信仰容忍度+0.50
每月属灵倾向+0.10
每月贵族政治倾向+0.10

In version 1.2, since vassals under the same overlord share a single cooldown for forced religious/cultural conversion, players can only force-convert one vassal every 10 years. Additionally, version 1.2 dynamically calculates the core status of territories; locations where the primary and accepted culture population falls below 50% will lose their core status. These new updates make expansion extremely slow and unrewarding, ultimately making the game quite boring due to its sluggish pacing.

To address these issues, this mod introduces the following changes:

Integrated Territories Overhaul: Modified the effects of the "Integrated" territory status. It now grants Maximum Control +15%, Monthly Control +0.05%, and Local Separatism +10%. The -10 Local Population Assimilation Speed debuff has been removed. This makes integrated territories function much closer to core territories.

Reduced Subject Conversion Cooldown: Reduced the cooldown for "Force Religion" and "Force Culture" on vassals to 3 days. (Note: The UI will still display 10 years, but the 3-day cooldown is what actually takes effect in-game).

Value Reworks: Following the Roman Empire DLC update, many players were highly dissatisfied with the effects of the Latin and Roman values. I have reworked their effects as follows:

Latinized:

Sell Efficiency: +2.50%

Expected Diplomatic Expenses: -20.00%

Monthly Humanism Change: +0.10

Monthly Plutocracy Change: +0.10

Allows the Latin Cultural Movement

Roman:

Stability Investment Cost: -20.00%

Tolerance of the True Faith: +0.50

Monthly Spiritualism Change: +0.10

Monthly Aristocracy Change: +0.10
