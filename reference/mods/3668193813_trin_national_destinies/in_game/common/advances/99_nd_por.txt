# Portugal (POR): advance tree
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before designing (EU5 1.4):
#   vanilla tag and culture advances, three per age
#     (advances/country_por.txt): the navy of Dinis, Ourique, the Cistercian
#     clearances; the Sesmarias, the Lisbon entrepot, the first voyages; the
#     bombardeiros, the Carreira da India, the feitorias; the bandeirantes,
#     the spice monopoly, the ordenancas (as discipline); colonial governors,
#     the Restoration, royal absolutism; port wine, Pombal's administration,
#     the military academy. Plus the cacadores.
#   vanilla Iberian advances (advances/region_iberia.txt): the Reconquista,
#     the caravels and galleons, the lieutenancy, viceroyalties, the tercio,
#     the Bourbon naval reforms.
#   vanilla elsewhere: the Casa da India is a reform and a decision, the
#     Ordenacoes a reform, the Estudo Geral a law, the Lei Mental an estate
#     policy, the marines a naval law, the foros a town right, the Cape route
#     an expedition; the Misericordia, the Inquisition, Methuen, the gold of
#     Minas, the Jesuits and the Tower of Belem are flavour events.
#   the mod's Spain (99_nd_spa.txt), which a country that goes on from
#     Portugal holds beside this tree: military orders, missions, the house
#     of trade, the treasure fleets, the tercio, colonial plantations.
#
# None of that is repeated. What is left is the kingdom under the voyages:
# the king's pinewood and the merchants' purse that built the ships, the
# crossbowmen of the concelhos, the Cortes that made a king, the tower that
# kept his parchments, the nau, the horse of the Moroccan fortresses, the
# cane mill, the house of mercy, the sea passes of the Indian Ocean, the
# three lines of militia, the cosmographers, the king who would come back,
# the bastions of Elvas, the terraces of the Douro, the tile kilns, the
# rebuilt capital, the royal factories, the school of commerce and the lines
# before Lisbon.
#
# No key below repeats, in the same age, a key that a vanilla POR,
# Portuguese or Iberian advance grants, nor one of the Algarve's (the other
# road into the tag): the per-age blocklist of scripts/creator_context.py POR.
#
# Stepping stone toward Spain: 4 or 5 per age.
# 29 advances, 16 of them unlocks (55 percent).

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
#
# The two parties of the formation event, each followed to its end.
# Path A, the crusade across the strait: Ceuta (1415), the northern towns
# that made Afonso V "the African" (1471), then the Atlantic ports of the
# Dukkala and the Sus and the tribute of the tribes behind them (1505 to
# 1515). Every stage is a conquest from Morocco.
# Path B, the Ocean Sea: the empty islands (Madeira, the Azores), the gold
# of Guinea (Arguin, the Mine), the pepper of India sold in Lisbon. Every
# place but Goa is reached by settlement, and the last stage is measured in
# the capital's market and in Lisbon itself.
# What is deliberately not here: the Feitoria, the Casa da India and the
# Cape route are vanilla's (an advance, a reform with its decision, an
# expedition); Granada, Gibraltar, Algeciras, Melilla and the Canaries are
# the objectives of the mod's Castile; the Americas, Manila and Seville are
# those of its Spain. No stage names an institution or a building of a
# later age, so both paths can be finished from the first age on
# (scripts/destiny/stage_floors.py POR prints no gap).

# Root. A kingdom whose three largest towns live on fish: the sardine of
# Setubal and Lagos, the tunny of the Algarve, the cod that would take its
# crews to the Banks. Both parties at court recruited from the same quays.
nd_por_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = POR has_variable = nd_por_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = colonial_ambition
	global_fish_output_modifier = 0.1
}

# ----- Path A: the crusade across the strait -----

# A1. Ceuta, 21 August 1415: two hundred ships, the king and three of his
# sons, and a city taken in a day. The expedition was paid for with loans
# and a debased coinage, which is the gold clause. Ceuta is Moroccan on day
# one.
nd_por_dest_chave_do_estreito = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_por_destiny
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = POR has_variable = nd_por_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_por_path_a_tt
			has_variable = nd_por_path_a
		}
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:ceuta
		gold >= 1000
	}
	icon = cas_marine_infantry
	army_disembark_speed = 0.25
	war_score_vs_other_religion_efficiency = 0.1
}
