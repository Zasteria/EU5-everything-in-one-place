# Catalonia (CAT): advance tree
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before designing (EU5 1.4):
#   vanilla tag advances, one per age (advances/culture_catalan.txt):
#     cat_principatus_cathaloniae, cat_generalitat, cat_remenca,
#     cat_els_segadors, cat_principat_constitucionalista,
#     cat_renaixenca_catalana.
#   vanilla Catalan culture advances, one per age (same file): the maritime
#     realm, fleet ordinances, Valencian golden age, chartered merchant
#     companies, constellation cities, Mossos d'Esquadra.
#   vanilla Iberian advances (advances/region_iberia.txt): almogavars, late
#     almogavars, Catalan crossbowmen with the sometent levy (DLC), Catalan
#     galley.
#   vanilla elsewhere: the Consulate of the Sea is a naval law and a town
#     right, the Usatges are a town right, the Sindicat Remenca a decision and
#     privilege, the Taula de Canvi of Barcelona an event bank; the start
#     setup reserves a Corts Catalanes government reform for the Iberian DLC.
#   the mod's Aragonese Empire (99_nd_cda.txt), which a country that goes on
#     from Catalonia holds beside this tree: pactism, the Consolat de Mar, the
#     Catalan Company, the Catalan Atlas, Caspe, the Drassanes, the Llotja,
#     the Generalitat Hall, the Renaixenca, the cotton mills.
#
# None of that is repeated. What is left is the Principality at ground level:
# the law that calls every man to defend the land, Barcelona's charter and
# the towns that joined it as streets, the mountain forge, the woollens, the
# municipal bank, the estates' own customs, the freed farmstead, the lottery
# of offices, glass, the miquelets and their successors, the gunmakers of
# Ripoll, the Generalitat's galleys, brandy, paper, the guild militia, the
# vote of 1713, the merchants' schools, the Maresme's ships and the men who
# came back rich from Havana.
#
# A country formed from Aragon keeps Aragon's advances. No key below repeats,
# in the same age, a key that a vanilla CAT, Catalan or ARA advance grants
# (the per-age blocklist printed by scripts/creator_context.py CAT).
#
# Stepping stone toward the Aragonese Empire and Spain: 4 or 5 per age.
# 25 advances, 16 of them unlocks (64 percent).

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
# Post-formation objectives, written for the country that realistically holds
# this tag: a Catalonia that was released or broke away (the start setup gives
# CAT cores on 28 Catalan locations, 25 of them Aragon's in 1337), an Andorra
# that grew, or a Catalan duchy come home from Greece. Not a great power
# changing its name: by the level rule Aragon (ARA_f is level 2 on 1.4)
# probably cannot press the button at all.
#
# What the start setup makes of that (main_menu/setup/1337, checked on 1.4):
#   Perpinya, Prades, Puigcerda and the Balearics belong to Mallorca (MLL), a
#     vassal of Aragon (12_diplomacy.txt:243). No former starts with them.
#   Valencia, the Catalan-speaking strip of Aragon (Fraga 63 percent Catalan,
#     Benabarre 77, Valderrobres 68 in 06_pops.txt) and Oriola are Aragon's.
#   Alghero is Genoa's, 15 percent Catalan: Peter the Ceremonious peopled it
#     with Catalans only in 1354.
#   Andorra is its own country (ANR), guaranteed by Aragon and by France
#     (12_diplomacy.txt:247-248), so it is asked for late and may be held as
#     a subject.
#
# The formation fork is where sovereignty rests, and each path follows its
# answer to the end.
#   Path A (nd_cat_path_a, the sworn principality): a prince is what joins
#     lands that keep their own laws. He gathers the countries of the Catalan
#     tongue one oath at a time: the islands, Valencia, then the last rushes
#     of Muntaner's bundle. Conquest path. Its capstone unlocks the Pobla.
#   Path B (nd_cat_path_b, the government of the land): the Diputacio del
#     General taxes, borrows and keeps accounts. After the Counties, which
#     its own style names, the only land asked for is Andorra, at the end
#     and as a subject if the player prefers. The rest is public credit,
#     municipal banks, Barcelona as a city, the country's woollens and its
#     law in print. Its capstone unlocks the Taula del General. Each stage
#     waits on one age and no later: none for stage 1, banking (age 2) for
#     stage 2, the printing press (age 3, from 1437) for stage 3, so that the
#     path can end in the same span as path A, which waits on no age at all
#     (owner's ruling of 2026-10-02; the capstone had asked for manufactories
#     and brandy stills, both of age 5, and could not end before 1637).
#
# Not repeated from elsewhere: vanilla's cat_ advances and Catalan culture
# advances (culture_catalan.txt), vanilla's lieutenancy, local governor and
# itinerant court buildings, the Barcelona monuments; the Aragonese Empire's
# destiny (Greece, the Aegean, Tunis, Alexandria, the Atlantic) and its
# Generalitat Hall; Occitania's (Cahors, Bordeaux, Bayonne, Navarre, Jaca,
# Huesca); the Regnum Gothorum's (Beziers, Agde, Lodeve, Nimes, the strait).
# No location is used by both paths.
#
# No kingdom-wide development anywhere. Barcelona's own growth is asked for
# on path B, from its 1337 value of 30.2 (scripts/development_estimate.py
# --explain barcelona): 45 at stage 2 (plus 50 percent), 60 at stage 3
# (doubled). History points at the city: it is the one place in the land
# whose growth the General's own taxes, bank and port were built to feed.

# The Peace and Truce sworn at Fondarella in 1173 is the first text to name
# the land by its bounds: from Salses to Tortosa and Lleida. Constitutions
# made by prince and estates together are the root of both paths, hence the
# speed of law-making.
nd_cat_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAT has_variable = nd_cat_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = laws

	legislative_efficiency = 0.05
}

# Stage 2B: the censals of the General. From the Corts of the 1360s the
# Diputacio funded itself by selling censals morts: a buyer pays a capital
# sum and receives a pension for ever, redeemable only when the debtor
# chooses. The game's government bond is the same instrument (no expiry,
# interest until redeemed), so the reward is a bond the country can sell two
# ages before central banks; selling one needs vanilla's banking advance,

# Stage 3B: lo General de Catalunya. The trading state of the fifteenth
# century, whose rule was to make cloth, sell it and keep the duties.
#   1422: the Corts' constitution Havents a cor shuts out foreign cloth.
#   1449: Alfonso the Magnanimous has a programme drawn up (Catalan woollens
#     favoured, cargoes in the Crown's own ships, more hulls built).
#   1453: the Busca, the party of merchants and craftsmen, takes the city
#     government of Barcelona on a programme of a cheaper coin and
#     protection, against the Biga of honoured citizens and importers of
#     fine cloth.
#   1480-1481: the Corts under Ferdinand II vote protection for the country's
#     trade and industry; in 1497, 1504 and 1506 royal orders put duties on
#     foreign cloth entering Sicily and Naples.
#   1484: the Llibre del Consolat de Mar is first printed, at Barcelona
#     (again in 1494; in Italian in 1519, Castilian in 1539, French in 1577).
#   1495: the Constitutions are printed, with the Usatges.
# Sources, read 2026-10-02: Gran Enciclopedia Catalana, entries "la Busca",
# "la Biga", "Ferran II de Catalunya-Arago", "Llibre del consolat de mar",
# and its history chapters "La polemica conjuntura del regnat d'Alfons el
# Magnanim", "L'economia: produir i comerciar", "Cap a una unio de corones";
# for the Biga's importers es.wikipedia "La Busca y la Biga"; for the 1495
# printing ca.wikipedia "Constitucions catalanes".
# The printing press is the institution of that state (age 3, from 1437):
# its sea law and its Constitutions went to the press within eleven years of
# each other. The workshops are the Obrador de Paraires (unlocked in age 2),
# whose cloth the bolla seals; four, as stage 2 asks for four tables, out of
# seven places in the Principality that start as a town or a city. Andorra
# is asked for here and not earlier because Aragon and France both guarantee
# it at the start; a subject Andorra counts. Unlocks the Taula del General.
# (DHE event nd_cat.13, branching)
