# Castile (CAS): advance tree
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before designing (EU5 1.4). Vanilla covers Castile more heavily than
# almost any other country, and all of this is left alone:
#   vanilla tag and culture advances (advances/country_cas.txt): the
#     repoblaciones, a land of castles, the Camino de Santiago, the navy, the
#     royal chronicles, the Santa Hermandad, the masters of the orders, the
#     audiencias, the New World, the marine infantry, the Armada Real, the
#     millones, the pasos, the Council of Castile, the Real Academia, the
#     Canal de Castilla, the royal arsenals and the royal factories.
#   vanilla Iberian advances (advances/region_iberia.txt): the Reconquista,
#     the lieutenancy, the caravels and galleons, the Lanzas de Castilla, the
#     tercio, the viceroyalties, the naval reforms.
#   vanilla elsewhere: the Mesta is a law with its own decisions, the Siete
#     Partidas, the Ordenamiento de Alcala, the Laws of Toro and the Nueva
#     Recopilacion are law policies, the alarde is a levy law policy, the
#     Cortes and the Comuneros are distribution of power policies, the fueros
#     are town rights, the caballeros villanos and the regimiento are estate
#     privileges, the Casa de Contratacion, the Nueva Planta, the intendancy
#     and the royal factories are reforms, the Order of the Band is a
#     chivalric order, and flavour events tell the behetrias, Nebrija's
#     grammar, the Complutense, the School of Salamanca, the arbitristas and
#     the Catastro of Ensenada. The Iberian DLC reserves the jinetes and the
#     caballeria de cuantia.
#   the mod's Spain (99_nd_spa.txt), which a Castile that goes on holds
#     beside this tree: the Mesta privilege, the military orders, fueros and
#     Cortes, jinetes, the wool market of Burgos, the fairs of Medina del
#     Campo, the Catholic Monarchs, rodeleros, the tercio, the economic
#     societies, the guerrilla.
#
# None of that is repeated. What is left is how the kingdom was run and paid
# for: the frontier councils and their militias, the king's paid vassals, the
# salt monopoly, the customs of Seville, the chancery's Castilian, the sales
# tax, the galleys, the town crossbowmen, the colleges, the embassy to Timur,
# the annuities and the accountants, the handgunners and bombards of Granada,
# the ordinance foot, the sword makers, the corregidores, the grain banks,
# the playhouses, the great squares, the general militia, the potters, the
# survey of the towns, the five guilds, the provincial militia, the foot
# guards, the novatores, the colonies of the Sierra Morena, the bank of 1782,
# the provincial grenadiers, the sword factory and the free trade in grain.
#
# No key below repeats, in the same age, a key that a vanilla CAS, Castilian
# or Iberian advance grants (the per-age blocklist printed by
# scripts/creator_context.py CAS).
#
# Stepping stone toward Spain and the Regnum Gothorum: 4 to 6 per age.
# 31 advances, 24 of them unlocks (77 percent).

# Between the Duero and the Tagus the land was held by councils, not lords:
# a walled town and the villages of its district under one fuero, governed
# by the householders and bound to march behind the town's banner. Whoever
# settled under the fuero owed the muster, Frank, Mozarab or Mudejar alike,
# which is the wrong_culture_levy_size line. Unlocks the Milicia Concejil.
nd_cas_concejos_de_villa_y_tierra = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAS }
	requires = city_building_advance
	icon = fortified_towns

	unlock_levy = levy_nd_cas_milicia_concejil
	wrong_culture_levy_size = 0.1

	ai_weight = { add = 20 }
}

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
# Post-formation objectives. The formation fork is who the king governs
# through, and each path follows its answer to the end.
#   Path A (nd_cas_path_a, the king's justice): the Crown rules through the
#     towns' money and its own judges. One war, the one the towns paid for
#     (Algeciras and Gibraltar, the Strait), then no land at all: colleges,
#     Valladolid as the seat of the courts, the corregidores, the treasury.
#     Its capstone unlocks the Real Chancilleria. Floors: none for stage 1,
#     the colleges (age 2) for stage 2, the corregidores (age 3) for stage 3.
#   Path B (nd_cas_path_b, the host of the ricoshombres): the frontier is
#     the great houses' business. Antequera and Huescar, the kingdom of
#     Granada, then what the lords took across the water: Melilla and the
#     islands of lordship. Conquest path with no age floor (vanilla's
#     colonies advance is of age 1). No building: nothing the lords built on
#     the frontier is distinct from a castle, which vanilla has.
# The paths end two ages apart at most (scripts/destiny/stage_floors.py).
#
# What the start setup makes of it (main_menu/setup/1337, checked on 1.4):
#   Algeciras, Gibraltar, Ronda and Marbella are Morocco's (MOR); Melilla too.
#   Antequera, Huescar, Malaga, Granada and the rest of the Granada area are
#     Granada's (GRA), a tributary of Castile. A tributary is sovereign, so
#     its land does not count; a Granada made a vassal does, which is what
#     Castile tried for two centuries.
#   The Canary Islands belong to nobody (CNR is a society of pops).
#   Valladolid is Castile's from day one, so it is asked for only as growth
#     from its 1337 development of 28 (scripts/development_estimate.py
#     --explain valladolid): 42 at stage 2 (plus 50 percent), 56 at stage 3
#     (doubled). History points at the city: the Audiencia sat there for good
#     from 1442 and the town lived off its lawsuits.
#
# Not repeated from elsewhere: vanilla's CAS, Castilian and Iberian advances
# and its lieutenancy, viceroyalty and supreme court buildings; the heritage
# tree above; the mod's Spain (Naples, Milan, Antwerp, Rome, Tunis, Palermo,
# Havana, Tenochtitlan, Manila, Cuzco, Seville's growth, the Escorial and the
# Real Audiencia) and Al-Andalus (Toledo, Cordoba, Toulouse, Palermo, Fez,
# Tlemcen, Tunis, Marrakesh, Tripoli, Cairo). No modifier key of Spain's
# destiny advances is used in an advance here. No location is used by both
# paths. No kingdom-wide development, no army size, no cabinet seat.
#
# Sources read 2026-10-02 (es.wikipedia): "Sitio de Algeciras (1342-1344)",
# "Real Audiencia y Chancilleria de Valladolid", "Conquista de las islas
# Canarias", "Toma de Antequera", "Rodrigo Manrique", "Pedro de Estopinan".
# From memory, not fetched: the Cortes of Toledo of 1480 (the reduction of
# the juros, the letrado majority on the Council), the toga and capa y espada
# corregidores, the capitulations of Granada and the repartimientos.

# Alfonso XI was declared of age at fourteen, at Valladolid in 1325, and the
# regents who had fought each other over him for thirteen years went home.
# A king who rules in person is the root of both paths.
nd_cas_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAS has_variable = nd_cas_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = crown_rights_laws_advance

	stability_cost_efficiency = 0.1
}

# PATH A: the king's justice (the towns' money and the king's judges)

# Stage 1A: the siege of Algeciras, 3 August 1342 to 26 March 1344. The
# Cortes of Burgos voted the alcabala to pay for it, the Pope and the king of
# France lent florins, the king pawned his crown, and a Genoese fleet under
# Egidio Boccanegra shut the harbour and threatened to sail home when its
# wages ran four months late. Alfonso died of the plague before Gibraltar in
# 1350. Both towns are Morocco's at the start. The gold is the war chest.
# (DHE event nd_cas.10)
nd_cas_dest_cerco_de_algeciras = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_cas_destiny
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAS has_variable = nd_cas_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_cas_path_a_tt
			has_variable = nd_cas_path_a
		}
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:algeciras
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:gibraltar
		gold >= 1000
	}
	icon = reconquista_of_the_sea

	total_loan_capacity_modifier = 0.3
	navy_maintenance_efficiency = 0.1
}

# Stage 3A: the Cortes of Toledo, 1480. The Crown took back about half of
# the rents granted away since 1464, gave the letrados the majority of the
# Royal Council and sent a corregidor to every city. The corregidores are
# the heritage advance of age 3; the gold is the treasury the resumption
# refilled. Unlocks the Real Chancilleria. global_nobles_estate_power is
# granted as a negative, as vanilla's own advances grant it: less power for
# the great houses is the reward. (DHE event nd_cas.12, branching)
nd_cas_dest_cortes_de_toledo = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_cas_dest_los_letrados
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAS has_variable = nd_cas_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		has_advance = nd_cas_corregidores
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_cas_dest_valladolid_56_tt
			location:valladolid = { owner ?= root development >= 56 }
		}
		prestige >= 75
		gold >= 3000
	}
	icon = cabinet_laws_advance

	unlock_building = nd_cas_dest_chancilleria
	global_nobles_estate_power = -0.1
	monthly_rebel_growth = -0.0005
	global_pop_promotion_speed_modifier = 0.1
}

# PATH B: the host of the ricoshombres (the frontier and beyond)
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Stage 1B: the frontier. Between royal campaigns the war with Granada was
# the business of the houses that held the march: cavalcades, castles taken
# by escalade, cattle and captives. Antequera fell on 24 September 1410 to
# the infante Fernando and the lords who rode with him; Huescar, at the
# other end of the line, to Rodrigo Manrique in 1434. Both are Granada's at
# the start. (DHE event nd_cas.13)
nd_cas_dest_la_frontera = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_cas_destiny
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAS has_variable = nd_cas_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_cas_path_b_tt
			has_variable = nd_cas_path_b
		}
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:antequera
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:huescar
		prestige >= 50
	}
	icon = border_raiders

	amount_looted_modifier = 0.33
	army_movement_speed = 0.05
}

# Stage 2B: the war of Granada, 1482 to 1492. It began as a lord's feat (the
# Marquis of Cadiz took Alhama by surprise) and the great houses led its
# hosts to the end. Malaga fell in 1487, Granada on 2 January 1492. The
# share measures the stage's own land: 15 of the 20 locations of the Granada
# area, counting subject land exactly as the location lines do.
# (DHE event nd_cas.14, branching)
nd_cas_dest_guerra_de_granada = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_cas_dest_la_frontera
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAS has_variable = nd_cas_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:granada
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:malaga
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_cas_dest_granada_share_tt
			area:granada_area = {
				any_location_in_area = {
					percent >= 0.75
					root = { owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = prev }
				}
			}
		}
		gold >= 2000
	}
	icon = nav_memories_reconquista

	global_levy_recruitment_speed_modifier = 0.1
	global_war_score_efficiency = 0.1
}

# Stage 3B: the islands of lordship. What Castile took beyond the water
# before 1500 was largely taken by lords. Henry III gave Jean de Bethencourt
# the right of conquest in 1402; Lanzarote (Teguise), Fuerteventura
# (Maxorata) and La Gomera (Hipalan) stayed islands of lordship under the
# Peraza and Herrera while the Crown conquered the other three itself, and
# those three are left out on purpose. Melilla was occupied on 17 September
# 1497 by Pedro de Estopinan with the Duke of Medina Sidonia's men. The
# islands belong to nobody at the start and Melilla is Morocco's.
# army_tradition_decay is granted as a negative: slower decay is the reward.
# (DHE event nd_cas.15, branching)
nd_cas_dest_islas_de_senorio = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_cas_dest_guerra_de_granada
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = CAS has_variable = nd_cas_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:melilla
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:teguise
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:maxorata
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:hipalan
		prestige >= 75
	}
	icon = maj_island_colonies

	subject_loyalty = 5
	colonial_maintenance_efficiency = 0.2
	army_tradition_decay = -0.001
	global_manpower_modifier = 0.1
}
