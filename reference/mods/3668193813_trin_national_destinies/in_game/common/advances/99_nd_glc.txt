# Galicia (GLC): advance tree
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before designing (EU5 1.4):
#   vanilla tag advances, one per age (advances/culture_galician.txt): the
#     kingdom of Garcia, the cathedral of Santiago, the Armada de Galicia, the
#     Atlantic walls, the Junta General, the Atlantic squadron.
#   vanilla Galician culture advances, one per age (same file): the rias, the
#     pilgrimages of the Camino, the foros, the legacy of the Irmandinos, the
#     mountain minerals, the Rexurdimento.
#   vanilla Iberian advances (advances/region_iberia.txt): the Reconquista,
#     the lieutenancy, the caravels and galleons, the viceroyalties, the
#     tercio, the naval reforms.
#   vanilla elsewhere: the Tower of Hercules is a monument at A Coruna
#     (unique_buildings.txt:4342), the pilgrimage to Santiago is an expedition
#     (expedition_types/pilgrimage_expedition.txt), the Camino is a Castile
#     advance and a culture event, the Fuero Juzgo a town right.
#   the mod: Leon (99_nd_lon.txt) has the Frankish boroughs of the pilgrim
#     road, maize and the raised granary, linen, the forges and the Asturian
#     alarm of 1808; Castile has the concejo militias and the salt monopoly;
#     Portugal the king's pine wood; Valencia the hemp walk and the coast
#     towers; Spain the military orders, the arsenals reform and the guerrilla.
#
# None of that is repeated. What is left is the kingdom at ground level: the
# grain owed to the Apostle from Galicia to the Rioja, the host of the mitre
# and its staff-bearer, the songs of the sea of Vigo, the jet carvers at the
# cathedral door, the seine of the Pontevedra mariners, the wine the monks
# sold to England, the lord who built seven bridges and the towers of his
# kind, the duke who was king at Ourense, the brotherhood that pulled 130
# castles down, a grammar school for the poor, the House of Spices, the
# king's court of appeal, the woman on the breach at A Coruna, the clinker
# shipwrights and their privateers, the brothers who sailed round the Horn,
# the Cardinal's college, the pazos, the granite masons, the dockyards of
# Ferrol and their twelve Apostles, the reapers who walked to Castile, the
# mail packets to Havana, the Catalan salters, Sargadelos, and the peasants
# who took Vigo back with a battalion of students beside them.
#
# The Irmandinos appear twice in the game and on purpose: vanilla's culture
# advance of age 4 is their LEGACY (manpower, production); this tree's age 3
# advance is the rising itself (a levy, and an event that can end the lords'
# towers for good).
#
# No key below repeats, in the same age, a key that a vanilla GLC, Galician
# or Iberian identity advance grants (the per-age blocklist printed by
# scripts/creator_context.py GLC).
#
# Stepping stone toward Spain: 4 or 5 per age.
# 25 advances, 16 of them unlocks (64 percent).

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
#
# The two heads of the kingdom, as the formation event puts the question.
# Path A, the patrimony of the Apostle: the church province Compostela was
# given in 1120 with the rights of Merida. Braga first, the older
# metropolis whose relics Gelmirez carried off in 1102; then the Leonese
# suffragans Ciudad Rodrigo and Coria; then the Portuguese ones the see
# kept until 1393, Lamego, Guarda and Evora. Conquest, with Santiago grown
# at each step.
# Path B, the king's town: what A Coruna was for. The mariners' seines and
# the port grown, then the House of Spices built and the New World known,
# then Tidore, where the one ship of Loaisa's fleet that crossed the
# Pacific from A Coruna made land in 1526, and its cloves sold at home.
# Buildings, growth and trade; one place far away, which a colony or a
# trade company may hold.
# No objective is a place inside the Galicia area by ownership alone: a
# Galicia released by Castile holds all of it on the first day. Santiago
# and A Coruna are asked for by name with growth or rank, never as the
# capital, and no stage reads the Torre do Senor.
# Not repeated from the sibling trees: Castile's and Leon's targets lie
# south and east of these, Portugal's are overseas and in Morocco, Spain's
# Havana and Manila are left to Spain.
# Earliest ages (scripts/destiny/stage_floors.py): A 1 2 2, B 2 3 3.

nd_glc_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = GLC has_variable = nd_glc_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = crown_rights_laws_advance

	diplomatic_reputation = 1
}

# --- Path A: the patrimony of the Apostle ---

# Santiago's development is 20 in 1337 (scripts/development_estimate.py
# --explain santiago_compostela); 25 is a quarter more. Braga is Portugal's
# on the first day.

	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_glc_destiny
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = GLC has_variable = nd_glc_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_glc_path_a_tt
			has_variable = nd_glc_path_a
		}
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:braga
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_glc_dest_santiago_25_tt

	}

	global_migration_attraction = 0.1
}
