# Aragon (ARA): advance tree
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before designing (EU5 1.4):
#   vanilla tag advances (advances/country_ara.txt): conquest of the Ebro,
#     Desperta Ferro, a Mediterranean trade power, the dinero jaques, the
#     Diputacion del General, overseas realms, Carinena vineyards, stronger
#     united, the Basilica del Pilar, protection of the little folk,
#     gunpowder souls, Zaragoza industry.
#   vanilla Aragonese culture advances (same file): the montaneses, the
#     Pyrenean passes, the frontier castles, the royal salt works, the
#     Sociedad Economica, foralism.
#   vanilla Catalan culture advances and Iberian advances (culture_catalan.txt,
#     region_iberia.txt): galleys, fleet ordinances, almogavars, crossbowmen,
#     caravels, the tercio, the lieutenancy, viceroyalties.
#   vanilla elsewhere: the Justicia de Aragon, the General Privilege and the
#     formalized oath are estate privileges; the Fuero de Aragon is a town
#     right; the Pyrenean fueros and the Crown of Aragon are government
#     reforms; the coronation at Zaragoza is a decision; the War of the Union
#     is a disaster; the chronicler, the Alterations of 1591, Sigena, Gracian
#     and the artillery arsenal are flavour events.
#   the mod's Aragonese Empire (99_nd_cda.txt), Catalonia (99_nd_cat.txt),
#     Spain (99_nd_spa.txt) and Al-Andalus (99_nd_adu.txt): pactism, Caspe,
#     the Consolat, the Llotja, the Generalitat, the Mesta, the tercios, the
#     acequia, insaculation and the rest of the sea-facing Crown.
#
# None of that is repeated. What is left is the inland kingdom at ground
# level: the villages of the southern march that governed themselves, the
# stock breeders of Zaragoza and their judge, the knight-fees of the great
# barons, the Mudejar builders and potters, the four arms of the Cortes, the
# town hosts of the war with Castile, saffron, the Aragonese pope, the school
# of Huesca, the German printers of Zaragoza, alabaster, the swordsmiths, the
# book of the kingdom's customs, the kingdom's own guard, the writ of
# manifestation, the Morisco vassals, the palaces of Renaissance Zaragoza,
# the tercios the Cortes paid for, Lastanosa's cabinet, the protectionists,
# the powder mills of Villafeliche, Pignatelli's canal, the first light
# infantry of the Spanish army, the two sieges, the academy of San Luis and
# the Aragonese party at court.
#
# No key below repeats, in the same age, a key that a vanilla ARA, Aragonese
# or Catalan advance grants (the per-age blocklist printed by
# scripts/creator_context.py ARA, plus the six Aragonese culture advances).
# No key is granted by more than one advance of this tree.
#
# Stepping stone toward the Aragonese Empire and Spain: 4 or 5 per age.
# 26 advances, 16 of them unlocks (62 percent).

# The communities of villages of Calatayud, Daroca, Teruel and Albarracin. In
# the thirteenth century the villages of the southern march won their
# separation from the towns that had ruled them: grouped in sesmas, governed
# by a general assembly (the plega), taxed by themselves and seated in the
# Cortes in their own right. Unlocks the Casa de la Comunidad.
# peasants_estate_levy_size is the size of the levies the peasants' estate
# raises: the villages owed the frontier their own companies.
# (DHE event nd_ara.2)

	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ARA }
	requires = codified_laws
	icon = nav_chartered_councils

	unlock_building = nd_ara_casa_de_la_comunidad

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
# Post-formation objectives. Written for the country that holds this tag in
# practice: the Aragon of 1337, which carries the tag from day one and owns
# the whole Aragon area, 25 of 29 Catalan locations, all of Valencia, half of
# Sardinia, and Mallorca as a vassal (main_menu/setup/1337, checked on 1.4).
# Nothing below asks for any of that land. A Catalan or Aragonese country
# that forms Aragon by the button meets the same objectives from further away.
#
# The formation fork is the kingdom's oldest quarrel, the one the Union of
# 1283 was sworn over: is Aragon the land the king rules from, or the title
# he rules by?
#   Path A (nd_ara_path_a, the Kingdom in Its Own Land): the realm of
#     Alfonso the Battler, king of Aragon and Pamplona, put back together and
#     governed from Zaragoza. Its land objectives are his: Molina (1129),
#     Soria (repeopled 1109 to 1114), Tudela (1119), Pamplona. Its weight is
#     not conquest: village communities, Mudejar kilns, sword forges and
#     Zaragoza grown from 30 development to 45 and then 60
#     (scripts/development_estimate.py --explain zaragoza gives 30 for 1337).
#     Its capstone unlocks the Aljaferia.
#   Path B (nd_ara_path_b, Head of the Crown): the house gathers realms
#     across the sea, each kept by its own law, and a subject's land counts.
#     The papal kingdom of Sardinia and Corsica (1297), Sicily brought back
#     to the senior line (1409), then Alfonso the Magnanimous's reach: Gaeta,
#     Malta and Skanderbeg's Kruje. Its capstone unlocks the Castillo Aragones.
#
# Earliest ages (scripts/destiny/stage_floors.py ARA): path A 1, 1, 3 (the
# Espaderia is an age 3 building), path B 1, 1, 2 (the Renaissance).
#
# Kept apart from the mod's other Iberian trees on purpose. Catalonia asks
# for the Balearics, Valencia, Alghero, Perpignan, Andorra and Barcelona's
# growth; the Aragonese Empire for Greece, the Aegean, Cyprus, Alexandria,
# Tunis, the Atlantic islands and the Caribbean, with Naples, Barcelona,
# Palma and Valencia grown; Spain for Naples, Milan, Antwerp, Rome, Tunis,
# Palermo and the Indies. No location and no modifier key of this tree is
# asked for or granted by any of the three, nor by Aragon's own heritage
# advances and formation choices.
# No cabinet seat, no army size test (the kingdom's army is its levies), no
# kingdom-wide development.

# Root: the oath. No king of Aragon was acknowledged until he had sworn the
# Fueros before the Justicia in La Seo. global_separatism is a key where a
# negative value is the benefit.
nd_ara_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ARA has_variable = nd_ara_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = crown_rights_laws_advance

	global_separatism = -0.05
}

# Stage 1: the Extremadura of Aragon. The southern march was held by its own
# villages, and its last piece is Molina, which Alfonso the Battler took in
# 1129 and which gave itself to Peter the Ceremonious in 1369 rather than
# accept Bertrand du Guesclin as its lord (it was handed back at Almazan in
# 1375 and has been called Molina de Aragon ever since).
# The Casa de la Comunidad is the heritage tree's age 1 building; it stands
# only in Aragonese-majority countryside and towns, of which the kingdom has
# 26, and history counts four communities: Calatayud, Daroca, Teruel and
# Albarracin.
# plateau_proximity_impact is a key where a negative value is the benefit.
nd_ara_dest_extremadura = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_ara_destiny
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ARA has_variable = nd_ara_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_ara_path_a_tt
			has_variable = nd_ara_path_a
		}
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:molina_de_aragon
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_ara_dest_comunidades_tt
			any_owned_location = {
				count >= 4
				has_building_with_at_least_one_level = nd_ara_casa_de_la_comunidad
			}
		}
	}
	icon = cas_repoblaciones

	levy_recovery_modifier = 0.1
	plateau_proximity_impact = -0.15
}

# Stage 2: the court at Zaragoza. A king who lives in Aragon holds the

# Zaragoza starts at 30 development; half as much again is 45. The Alfar
# Mudejar is the heritage tree's age 1 kiln yard: Mudejar masters built the

# Stage 3: Aragon and Pamplona. From 1076 to 1134 one king ruled both, and

# needs iron in the local market, which Albarracin and Benabarre mine.
# Unlocks the Aljaferia.
# land_cost_on_distance_from_capital_speed_propagation is how fast the
# capital's proximity travels over land: one king's writ from Roncesvalles
# to Teruel. (Not tax_income_efficiency: vanilla's own dinero jaques grants
# it one age later.)
# army_tradition_decay is a key where a negative value is the benefit.
nd_ara_dest_aragon_y_pamplona = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_ara_dest_corte_en_zaragoza
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ARA has_variable = nd_ara_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:pamplona
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:tudela
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_ara_dest_zaragoza_60_tt
			location:zaragoza = {
				owner ?= root
				development >= 60
			}
		}
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_ara_dest_espaderias_tt
			any_owned_location = {
				count >= 3
				has_building_with_at_least_one_level = nd_ara_espaderia
			}
		}
		gold >= 3000
		prestige >= 75
	}
	icon = consolidate_the_royal_domain

	unlock_building = nd_ara_dest_aljaferia
	land_cost_on_distance_from_capital_speed_propagation = 0.1
	fort_limit_modifier = 0.1
	army_tradition_decay = -0.001
}

# Stage 1: the kingdom of Sardinia and Corsica, which Boniface VIII invented
# for James II in 1297. In 1337 the crown holds Cagliari, Sassari and the
# east; Arborea holds Oristano (broken at Sanluri in 1409, its rights bought
# in 1420), Genoa holds the Doria rock of Castelsardo (taken in 1448 and
# renamed Castillo Aragones) and all Corsica, where Alfonso V besieged
# Bonifacio in 1420 and the crown never ruled. A subject's land counts, so
# Arborea may be bound instead of broken.
nd_ara_dest_cerdena_y_corcega = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_ara_destiny
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ARA has_variable = nd_ara_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		custom_tooltip = {
			text = nd_ara_path_b_tt
			has_variable = nd_ara_path_b
		}
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:oristano
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:castelsardo
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:bonifacio
	}
	icon = sar_the_giudicati

	subject_opinions = 10
	own_coast_naval_combat_bonus = 0.1
}

# Stage 2: Trinacria. Sicily had kings of the house's cadet line from 1296;
# Martin the Younger landed with an army in 1392, and when he died in 1409
# the island passed to his father and to the crown for good. Messina,
# Catania and Syracuse are the east coast the barons held against him.
nd_ara_dest_trinacria = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_ara_dest_cerdena_y_corcega
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ARA has_variable = nd_ara_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:messina
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:catania
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:syracuse
		prestige >= 50
		gold >= 2000
	}
	icon = sic_sicilian_baroque

	diplomatic_capacity = 1
	global_merchant_capacity_modifier = 0.1
}

# Stage 3: the Magnanimous. Alfonso V made Gaeta his base on the mainland in
# the 1430s, kept a humanist court in Italy and never came home; Malta was
# the county at the end of his Sicilian kingdom; and by the Treaty of Gaeta
# (26 March 1451) Skanderbeg became his vassal and a royal garrison under
# Bernard Vaquer took over the castle of Kruje. A vassal's land counts, as it
# did then. Naples itself is left to the Spain and Aragonese Empire trees.
# Unlocks the Castillo Aragones.
# accepted_culture_maintenance_cost_modifier is a cost key: a negative value
# is the benefit.
nd_ara_dest_el_magnanimo = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	requires = nd_ara_dest_trinacria
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ARA has_variable = nd_ara_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = {
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:gaeta
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:malta
		owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns = location:kruje
		has_embraced_institution = institution:renaissance
		gold >= 3000
		prestige >= 75
	}
	icon = ues_strong_dynastic_ties

	unlock_building = nd_ara_dest_castillo_aragones
	power_projection = 5
	accepted_culture_maintenance_cost_modifier = -0.1
	diplomatic_range_modifier = 0.25
}
