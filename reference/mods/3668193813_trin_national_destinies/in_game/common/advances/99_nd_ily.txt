##############################################################################
# Illyria (ILY): National Destinies Advance Tree
#
# 33 heritage advances, 17 of them unlocking tangible content.
#
# THE THESIS. This is not the famous Illyria. The Napoleonic Illyrian
# Provinces (1809) and the romantic Illyrian Movement (1830s) are out of
# period and appear nowhere in this tree. What is modelled is the learned and
# ecclesiastical Illyria of roughly 1450 to 1650: the self-designation of the
# Catholic Slavs of the eastern Adriatic, who claimed the classical Illyrian
# inheritance as humanist and Roman pedigree. Vinko Pribojevic of Hvar
# (oration 1525, printed Venice 1532), Mavro Orbini of Ragusa (Il regno degli
# Slavi, 1601), Bartol Kasic (Institutionum linguae Illyricae, Rome 1604),
# San Girolamo degli Illirici in Rome (papal grant 1453, Sixtus V's rebuild
# and Sapientiam Sanctorum 1589) and the Illyrian College at Loreto (Gregory
# XIII, 1580).
#
# THE SIGNATURE. Innocent IV's 1248 grant to Bishop Filip of Senj licensed
# the Slavonic liturgy in Glagolitic letters, justified by the false but
# load-bearing attribution of the script to St Jerome, the littera
# Hieronymiana. That attribution is exactly why the Roman institution is San
# GIROLAMO. A Catholic state celebrating Mass in a Slavic vernacular in a
# non-Latin alphabet is unique in Europe, and this tree is built on it: the
# parish (age 1), the press (age 3), the grammar (age 4), the seminary and
# the lexicon (age 6). The mechanical expression is rural clergy: Illyria
# grows priests in villages, and priests are its instrument of literacy,
# conversion and control.
#
# WHAT IT DELIBERATELY IS NOT. The Habsburg chanceries later used Gens
# Illyrica for the ORTHODOX Serb settlers of southern Hungary under the
# Leopoldine privileges of 1690. That strand belongs to SER and is absent
# here. Dalmatia (DAL) holds the maritime city republic: the communal
# statutes, the Ragusan quarantine of 1377, the argosies, the uskoks. Illyria
# is the consolidation inland, and its economy is portage, not seafaring: the
# Pannonian interior joined to the Adriatic by the tricesima stations, the
# Split scala and the mountain roads.
##############################################################################

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
#
# Post-formation mission tree. Two paths of three stages, branching on the
# choice taken at the proclamation. Neither path restates the heritage tree:
# the heritage tree is the kingdom as it was, and these are the two things it
# could have become.
#
# PATH A, CONFINIUM, extends Confinia Regni. The cordon stops being a line to
# be held and becomes a line to be moved. The historical trajectory is the
# one the Croatian and Slavonian generalcies actually followed once the
# offensive turned after 1683: across the Drava into the sanjak that had
# been harvesting Slavonia since 1543, down to the Sava crossing and the
# confluence the chancery called Alba Graeca, and finally along the Danube
# into the Banat, where the Militaergrenze reached its full length from the
# Adriatic to Transylvania. The internal question is who owns the frontier's
# people, which is what the Statuta Valachorum of 1630 answered.
#
# PATH B, SACRUM ILLYRICUM CONTINUED. The mission becomes an institution.
# Propaganda Fide is founded in 1622 and its polyglot press cuts Illyrian
# type; the union accepted at Marca in 1611 turns the Orthodox garrison
# population of the frontier into Roman obedience without touching its rite;
# and Farlati begins the Illyricum Sacrum, a register of every see in
# Illyricum, at the moment the crown reaches the province the geographers
# still call by that name. Its objectives are institutions, presses, revenue
# and endowments first and conquest last.
#
# NOTHING NAVAL. The uskoks, the argosies and the Adriatic war belong to DAL
# and stay there, as in the heritage tree above.
##############################################################################

# The proclamation is entered in the register and the course is fixed.
nd_ily_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = ILY has_variable = nd_ily_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = crown_power_advance_renaissance

	stability_cost_efficiency = 0.15
}
