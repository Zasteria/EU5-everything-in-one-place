# Leon (LON): advance tree
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before designing (EU5 1.4):
#   vanilla tag advances, one or two per age (advances/culture_leonese.txt):
#     lon_kingdom_from_oviedo, lon_order_of_santiago, lon_canada_leonesa,
#     lon_cortes_de_leon, lon_frontier_expansion, lon_tierra_de_campos,
#     lon_adelantado_mayor_de_leon, lon_maragato_arrieros,
#     lon_bierzo_vineyards, lon_real_casa_moneda, lon_leonese_collieries,
#     lon_modern_brigades.
#   vanilla Leonese culture advances, one per age (same file): the Asturian
#     inheritance, Gothic art, the pendon morado, the textile industry, the
#     provincial academies, the industrial revival.
#   vanilla Asturias (ATU) advances, which a Leon formed from Asturias keeps:
#     the cradle of the Reconquista, Cantabrian fisheries, the mountain
#     fastness, cider orchards, the coal basin, steel works.
#   vanilla elsewhere: the Fuero Juzgo is a town right open to LON
#     (town_rights/15_iberia.txt:36); the Leyes de Toro are a policy that a
#     Spain event unlocks (laws/03_estate_laws.txt:82, flavor_cas.48); the
#     School of Salamanca is an event building that a Castile event raises
#     (event_only_buildings.txt:3700, flavor_cas.47) and a Spain advance
#     (spa_school_salamanca: research and diplomatic capacity). A Leonese
#     owner of Toro and Salamanca gets neither event, so this tree's own
#     events hand over the same vanilla policy and the same vanilla building
#     instead of writing second ones.
#   the mod's Spain (99_nd_spa.txt): the Mesta, the military orders, fueros
#     and Cortes, the jinete, Medina del Campo, the tercio. The mod's Regnum
#     Gothorum (99_nd_got.txt): the Liber Iudiciorum, the Hispanic rite.
#     The mod's Castile (99_nd_cas.txt): concejo militias, the alcabala,
#     colegios mayores, positos, plazas mayores, provincial militias.
#
# None of that is repeated. What is left is the kingdom at ground level and
# on its own terms: the charter of 1017, the kings in San Isidoro, the
# Frankish boroughs of the pilgrim road, the mountain foot, the Estudio, the
# village assembly called by the bell, the summer pastures, the heir's
# Principality and its Junta, the knights of the pear tree, the entail, the
# presses, the Comunidades, the doctors of money, Menendez's galleons, maize
# and the raised granary, the dry ports and the forts of the Portuguese
# border, linen, Feijoo's cell in Oviedo, the forges, Jovellanos's institute
# and his report, the shot foundry at Trubia, the Junta that declared war on
# Napoleon and the lancers of the Salamanca oak pasture.
#
# No key below repeats, in the same age, a key that a vanilla LON, Leonese,
# ATU or Iberian advance grants (the per-age blocklist printed by
# scripts/creator_context.py LON), on the formed road or the released one.
#
# Stepping stone toward the Regnum Gothorum and Spain: 4 or 5 per age.
# 26 advances, 15 of them unlocks (58 percent).

# The Fuero of 1017: Alfonso V and the full court in the cathedral of Leon,
# 48 precepts, twenty for the whole kingdom and twenty-eight for the city.
# The market held in peace every Wednesday, the house no officer may enter,
# dues lightened for the craftsman who comes to settle. The Fuero Juzgo
# itself is vanilla's town right; this is the settlers' charter beside it,
# so it pays in people who come and townsmen who stay content.
nd_lon_fuero_de_leon = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = LON }
	requires = codified_laws
	icon = laws

	global_migration_attraction = 0.1
	burghers_estate_target_satisfaction = small_permanent_target_satisfaction
}

# The Salamanca masters on money: Azpilcueta's commentary on exchange (1556)
# says coin is worth less where coin is plentiful, and Mercado and Molina
# reason out the just price from what buyers will freely give. Vanilla's
# Spain has the School as research and diplomacy (spa_school_salamanca);
# this is the part vanilla leaves out, the theory of prices, so it pays in
# steadier coin and cheaper imports. monthly_inflation is a key where a
# negative value is the benefit. (DHE event nd_lon.4, branching; the event
# raises vanilla's School of Salamanca building, which otherwise only a
# Castile event can.)
nd_lon_doctores_de_la_moneda = {
	age = age_4_reformation
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = LON }
	requires = early_modern_administation
	icon = spa_school_salamanca

	monthly_inflation = -0.001
	import_efficiency = tiny_trade_efficiency_bonus
}

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
#
# Two answers to what a king of Leon is, as the formation event asks it.
# Path A, the crown of the emperors: the Leonese Extremadura Alfonso IX took
# in 1229 and 1230, then Toledo and Burgos (the old capital the kings of Leon
# called themselves heirs of, and the county that broke away), then Cordoba
# and Baeza with two princes doing homage, as in 1135. Conquest and vassals.
# Path B, the kingdom of the Decreta: boroughs on the pilgrim road and the
# royal city grown, then the village councils and the Estudio of Salamanca,
# then the fair towns of the Tierra de Campos. Buildings, growth and trade.
# No objective is a place inside the Leon area by ownership alone: a Leon
# released by Castile may hold all of it on the first day.
# Earliest ages (scripts/destiny/stage_floors.py): A 1 1 1, B 1 2 3.

nd_lon_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = LON has_variable = nd_lon_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = frequent_councils

	global_estate_satisfaction_recovery = 0.001
}
