# ==========================================
# Tunis (TUN): advance tree.
#
# Vanilla 1.4 gives the tag 13 advances of its own and the Tunisian culture 6
# more (country_tun.txt), and the Maghrebi group and the Muslim faith give
# about 55 shared ones. Their subjects are NOT repeated here: the corsair
# galleys, the caliphal claim, the ghulam guard, the ribats of the Sahel, the
# treaties with the Christian ports, Ibn Khaldun's circle, the Andalusi refuge,
# the export monopolies, the Tunis to Tripoli axis, the Shadhili order, the
# admiralty, La Goulette, the two schools of law, the Husaynids, the
# Fundamental Pact and the Nahda. Vanilla's flavour events and decisions
# (events/DHE/flavor_tun.txt, decisions/flavor_decisions_tun.txt) tell the
# reunification of Ifriqiya under one emir, so nothing here does either.
#
# Tunis is also the first step toward Carthage (level 4) and the Almohads
# (level 3), whose trees and bureaucracies a Tunisian country may hold later:
# the Andalusi chancery, the chief Maliki judge, the maritime customs bureau,
# the ribat network, the corsair captains' guild, the kasbah, the funduq,
# the shipyard and the mobile court belong to them and are left alone.
#
# What is told here is what is left, and it is most of the country: the
# tribes of the steppe, the olive coast, the Zaytuna, the reef of Tabarka,
# the Turkish garrison, the Moriscos and their red caps, the renegades'
# ships, the captives, the mountaineers of the beys and the end of the corso.
#
# No key below is one a vanilla Tunis advance grants in the same age (the
# per-age blocklist of scripts/creator_context.py TUN), and no key appears
# in more than two advances. 4 per age, 15 of 24 unlock something.
# Values are vanilla's own advance scale on 1.4 (VANILLA_REFERENCE.md,
# "Advance Modifier Ranges").
# ==========================================

# === AGE 3: DISCOVERY (1437 to 1537): prosperity, then the two empires ===

# The red coral of Tabarka and Marsa al-Kharaz. The sultan of Tunis farmed
# the coral of Tabarka out to a Barcelona merchant, Rafael Vives, from 1446
# to 1448 and to Genoese from 1451 to 1506 (F. Fichet de Clairfontaine
# reviewing P. Gourdin, "Tabarka. Histoire et archéologie d'un préside
# espagnol et d'un comptoir génois", Archéologie médiévale 39, 2009, pp. 350
# to 353, https://journals.openedition.org/archeomed/18545). The lease of
# 1451 went to Clemente Cicero, consul of the Genoese at Tunis: ten years,
# every Hafsid shore west of Ras Jebel, 2,000 doubles a year (P. Gourdin,
# Mélanges de l'École française de Rome, Moyen Age 102, 1990, pp. 131 to
# 171). The Genoese grant of 1542, with Lomellini among its holders, was NOT
# a Hafsid one: Charles V gave it, by his treaty with the sultan after the
# taking of Tunis in 1535 (Fichet de Clairfontaine; D. Valérian's review,
# https://journals.openedition.org/remmm/7032), so the texts do not credit
# it to the Hafsids. Read 2026-10-01. The rent was the point, so the country
# learns to charge more at its gates.

# ===== DESTINY TREE =====
# Two roads out of the formation choice (nd_tun.1), one for each answer to the
# question of who holds the interior. A path variable gates stage 1 of each.
#
# Who holds what on day one, because the sitting Hafsids get this tree without
# ever forming (main_menu/setup/1337/10_countries.txt, 12_diplomacy.txt:263-271):
#   TUN itself: 37 locations, among them Tunis, Kairouan, the Sahel, Sfax,
#     Tabarka and Bizerte.
#   Non-sovereign subjects (vassals and familial governors, the subject types
#     with has_limited_diplomacy = yes): Bejaia, Annaba and Constantine (BIJ),
#     the Djerid (GFS), Gabes, Djerba and the Nafusa (GBS), Tripoli (TRP),
#     Touggourt (TGT).
#   Tributaries, which are sovereign (subject_types/tributary.txt:116) and so
#     do NOT count for owns_or_non_sovereign_subject_owns: Biskra (ZAB),
#     Ghadames (DJE), Misratah and Sirte (BTL), the Mzab (MZB).
#   Foreign: Tlemcen and Algiers (TLE), Malta (SAO, the county of Salona,
#     whose count was also count of Malta; a vassal of NEO, 12_diplomacy.txt:237).
# A newcomer holds three quarters of the Tunis and Tripolitania areas directly,
# so marks inside those two areas are paired with marks outside them or with
# thresholds, never left as a stage's only test.
#
# Path A, the Sultan of the Tribes: suzerainty carried by the tribes' cavalry.
#   Every mark counts when a vassal or a governor holds it, because ruling
#   through chiefs and kinsmen IS this road. The marks are the oases that paid
#   the Hafsids tribute (the Zab, Ghadames), the Syrtic steppe of the Dabbab,
#   Kairouan as the market of the steppe, and the homage of Tlemcen that Abu
#   Zakariya took in 1242 and Abu Faris in 1424.
# Path B, the Master of the Customs House: direct rule paid for by the ports.
#   Stage 1 asks DIRECT ownership of Tripoli and Djerba, which start under
#   annexable vassals: revoking a local dynasty's grant is the deed (Abu Faris
#   took Tripoli from the Banu Thabit in 1401). After that the road is not
#   conquest: Tunis grown from its 1337 development of 31
#   (scripts/development_estimate.py --explain tunis), the salaried army as an
#   institution, the treasury, a fleet, and one raiding mark, Malta, which a
#   Hafsid fleet besieged in 1429.
#
# What is deliberately NOT asked:
#   Direct ownership of anything a familial governor holds. That subject type
#   cannot be annexed (subject_types/familial_governor.txt, can_be_annexed = no)
#   and while the reform tun_strong_familial_governors stands a prince can be
#   granted any city but the capital (flavor_tun.14), so the test would hang on
#   vanilla's succession events and not on the player. Bejaia and Constantine
#   therefore count when a governor holds them.
#   Anything a vanilla decision already rewards: the caliphate (great power
#   standing and jurisprudence), Ifriqiya reunited (centralization, estates,
#   the end of the governors reform), the masters of the Sahara (26 desert
#   locations owned directly; one caravan gate here is not that).
#   Pantelleria and Lampedusa: both are impassable on the 1.4 map
#   (map_data/default.map:1208, location_templates.txt:3242), so an ownership
#   test on either is false forever.
#   The marks of Carthage's and the Almohads' own trees (Sardinia, Iberia,
#   Italy, Morocco, the western salt road), which a country that goes on to
#   form either one holds beside this tree.
#
# Rewards: 15 advance keys and 24 timed keys, none repeated, none granted by
# the heritage tree above, by any timed modifier of this country, or by a
# vanilla Tunis advance of age 1. Values are vanilla's advance scale
# (VANILLA_REFERENCE.md, "Advance Modifier Ranges").

# Root. The court at Tunis exchanged embassies with Aragon, Venice, Egypt and
# Kanem under either kind of ruler.
nd_tun_destiny = {
	age = age_1_traditions
	depth = 0
	potential = { has_or_had_tag = TUN has_variable = nd_tun_formed has_game_rule = nd_destinies_enabled }
	allow = { stability >= 75 }
	icon = maghrebi_administration
	monthly_diplomats = 0.05
}
