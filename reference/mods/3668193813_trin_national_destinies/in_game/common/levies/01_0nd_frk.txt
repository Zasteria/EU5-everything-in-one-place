# Francia levies unlocked in Age 5. The numeric prefix encodes the unlock
# age as vanilla does (01 = absolutism), so the age 6 vanilla levies still
# take precedence once they are unlocked.

# The ban of 808 bound free landholders, in the countryside and in the
# chartered towns alike, which is why burghers muster beside peasants.
# Laborers serve too: a levy that mixes peasants with burghers but refuses
# laborers sends them to the next valid levy, usually a vanilla generic one
# (Workshop report 2026-08-26; vanilla never leaves them out of such a mix).
# There is no culture gate on purpose: the capitulary bound every free man
# of the realm whatever his people, and that is the whole point of the ban.
levy_nd_frk_heerbann = {
	size = levy_generic_infantry_size
	unit = a_nd_frk_heerbann

	allowed_pop_type = peasants
	allowed_pop_type = laborers
	allowed_pop_type = burghers

	country_allow = {
		has_or_had_tag = FRK
	}
}
