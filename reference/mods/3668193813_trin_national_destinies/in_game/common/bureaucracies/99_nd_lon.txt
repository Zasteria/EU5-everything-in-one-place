# Leon (LON): bureaucracies
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before choosing subjects (EU5 1.4):
#   vanilla bureaucracies Leon can hold (bureaucracies/generic.txt, ten of
#     them, from Confessionalism or the age 4 advance): a board of revenue, a
#     privy council, an audit bureau, a colonial office, an admiralty, a
#     foreign ministry, a trade commission, a war council, an ordnance board
#     and a commissariat. None is repeated here by name or by subject.
#   vanilla Leon elsewhere: the Cortes de Leon are a reform it starts with
#     (lon_cortes_de_leon_reform) and an advance; the Adelantado Mayor de
#     Leon, the Order of Santiago, the Real Casa de Moneda, the canada, the
#     Maragato carriers and the pendon morado are advances; the Fuero Juzgo
#     is a town right; the Leyes de Toro are a policy and the School of
#     Salamanca a building. So no adelantado and no merino (the same office
#     under its older name), no mint, no Cortes, no court of the Fuero Juzgo.
#   the mod's Castile (99_nd_cas.txt): Alcaldes de Casa y Corte, Alfaqueques,
#     Consulado de Burgos, Guardas de Castilla. The mod's Spain: Mesta,
#     Corregimiento, Consejo Real, Casa de Contratacion. The mod's Aragon:
#     Sobrejunterias, Real Audiencia.
#   this country's own tree (99_nd_lon.txt): the Fuero of 1017, the pantheon,
#     the Frankish boroughs, the Estudio, the concejo abierto, the branas, the
#     Principality and its Junta General, Alcantara, the Leyes de Toro, the
#     presses, the Comunidades, the doctors of money, the dry ports, the
#     border forts, the Junta of 1808. Buildings: Panteon de los Reyes, Burgo
#     de Francos, Casa de Concejo, Brana, Horreo, Fabrica de Lienzos,
#     Instituto Asturiano, Fabrica de Trubia, Feria General.
#
# What is left, and what this kingdom had in its own name:
# 1. Notaria Mayor de Leon: the kingdom's own notary in the royal chancery,
#    kept apart from Castile's after the crowns were joined.
# 2. Moneda Forera: the hearth tax voted at Benavente in 1202 against the
#    king's promise not to debase the coin, with its seven-yearly rolls of
#    who pays and who is exempt.
# 3. Audiencia Escolastica: the court of the scholastic of Salamanca, judge
#    of every member of the Estudio.
# 4. Tenencia de las Torres de Leon: the wardenship of the royal towers of
#    the city, held at the king's pleasure.
#
# Estates. Both roads into the tag (a released Leon, Asturias) are Leonese
# Catholic monarchies with the same starting laws; the territory has all four
# estates on day one (882 thousand pops: peasants, burghers, clergy, nobles).
# The four offices align differently: crown and towns against the lords;
# lords and clergy against the countryfolk; clergy against the towns; crown
# and lords against the towns.
#
# Slots. Vanilla gives Leon none on the day it forms (a capital grants one
# only as a megalopolis, location_ranks/00_default.txt:50; Leon is a town).
# The auto modifier grants 2, half of these four. Vanilla adds one with the
# age 4 bureaucracy advance and one in age 6, and from Confessionalism its
# ten generic offices compete for the same slots.
#
# No cabinet seat and no character modifier. No key stands in both the funded
# and the neglected block of one institution. No government power key: none
# of these offices made a king more lawful. All IDs are lon_-prefixed.
#
# Dropped after research: the Juicio del Libro (the appeal court of Leon that
# judged by the Fuero Juzgo). No page fetched this session describes it, and
# its law book is vanilla's town right.

# 1. Notaria Mayor de Leon
# Fetched (es.wikipedia, "Notario mayor del reino (corona de Castilla)"): one
# notario mayor each for Castile, Leon, Toledo and Andalusia; counted among
# the officers of the crown's written administration beside the chancellor;
# in 1431, under John II, a notario mayor drew 98,400 maravedis a year.
# Fetched (es.wikipedia, "Notaria mayor de Andalucia"): created by Alfonso X
# in 1252 together with those of Castile and Leon; later tied by inheritance
# to one noble house.
# From memory, not fetched: that the notary of Leon checked and registered
# the charters issued for the kingdom, and that the towns of Leon asked in
# the Cortes for officers who were natives of the kingdom. The second is why
# the towns like the office. The lords dislike it for what it does when it
# works (a grant that is written, registered and can be looked up is harder
# to stretch) and profit when it does not: the fetched fate of the Andalusian
# notary, an heirloom of one house, is the neglected side here.

# 2. Moneda Forera
# Fetched (es.wikipedia, "Moneda forera"): first set as a tribute at the
# Cortes of Benavente in 1202, one maravedi a year from every free man for
# seven years, against the king's undertaking not to break the coin (mint it
# with less metal); canons and knights exempt; it became a tax on commoners
# with at least 120 maravedis of property, collected in the first and last
# year of each seven; for it the Padrones de Moneda Forera listed every
# householder as payer or as exempt hidalgo, redrawn every seven years; the
# yield dwindled to almost nothing and the tax was ended in 1724. The page's
# bibliography has a study of these rolls as a source for Asturias.
# From memory, not fetched: that in Asturias and the Leonese mountains most
# householders were entered as hidalgos, and that the rolls were the proof
# of nobility a family produced in court. Both are why the neglected side is
# a roll on which nobody pays.
# minting_inflation_threshold is how much a country can mint before the coin
# inflates (vanilla's minting office gives 0.02; its median grant is 0.01).
# The lords' levy on the funded side: the exemption was for those who serve
# in arms, and the roll is the list of who owes that service.
# Vanilla's Leon has a mint as an advance (lon_real_casa_moneda: interest and
# court spending) and this tree has the Salamanca doctors on prices; neither
# is the bargain of 1202 or its register.
lon_moneda_forera_bureaucracy = {
	implementation_price = price:implement_bureaucracy_price
	maintenance_price = price:maintain_bureaucracy_price
	removal_price = price:remove_bureaucracy_price
	# a roll redrawn by each council once in seven years, no standing office:
	# vanilla's light multiplier (0.003, the Bayt al-Mal and the Byzantine set)
	maintenance_price_modifier = {
		value = country_economical_base
		multiply = 0.003
	}

	potential = {
		has_or_had_tag = LON
		has_game_rule = nd_bureaucracies_enabled
	}

	allow = {}

	estates_that_like = {
		nobles_estate
		clergy_estate
	}
	estates_that_dislike = {
		peasants_estate
	}

	neutral_modifier = {
		minting_inflation_threshold = 0.01
	}

	positive_modifier = {
		scale = { value = scope:maintenance }
		peasants_estate_max_tax = 0.05
		nobles_estate_levy_size = 0.15
	}

	negative_modifier = {
		scale = {
			value = 1
			subtract = scope:maintenance
		}
		monthly_inflation = 0.002
		tax_income_efficiency = small_tax_income_efficiency_penalty
	}
}

# 3. Audiencia Escolastica
# Fetched (es.wikipedia, "Universidad de Salamanca"): the scholastic
# (maestrescuela) of the cathedral school is on record in 1174; Alfonso IX
# made the schools a Estudio General in 1218; Alexander IV's licence to teach
# everywhere in 1255; the constitutions of Martin V in 1422; a corporation
# independent of the city and of the chapter, jealous of its privileges,
# with its own rules "and even its own penal institutions".
# Fetched (es.wikipedia, "Maestrescuela"): the maestrescuela was chancellor
# of the university at Salamanca. Fetched (es.wikipedia, search result for
# "Padre Putas"): "the maestrescuela and judge of the Estudio". Fetched
# (es.wikipedia, "Archivo Historico de la Universidad de Salamanca"): the
# Audiencia Escolastica is one of the four bodies whose papers make up the
# university's archive.
# From memory, not fetched: that the 1422 constitutions are what gave the
# scholastic his jurisdiction over students, masters and their servants in
# civil and criminal suits, that the court had its own gaol, and the quarrels
# with the city's justice over students taken at night. The text names no
# date for the court itself.
# This tree's age 1 advance on the Estudio raises what clergy and nobles can
# learn, and vanilla's School of Salamanca is a building; this is the court
# that kept the scholars out of the town's hands. With the advance the funded
# side counts half as much again; the values are written for that case
# (0.06 and 0.15 at most).
# Gate: the city itself.
lon_audiencia_escolastica_bureaucracy = {
	implementation_price = price:implement_bureaucracy_price
