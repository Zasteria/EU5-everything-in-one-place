# Mallorca (MLL): bureaucracies
# National Destinies mod
#
# Read before choosing subjects (EU5 1.4):
#   vanilla bureaucracies Mallorca can hold (bureaucracies/generic.txt, ten of
#     them, from Confessionalism or the age 4 advance): a board of revenue, a
#     privy council, an audit bureau, a colonial office, an admiralty, a
#     foreign ministry, a trade commission, a war council, an ordnance board
#     and a commissariat. None is repeated by name or by subject, so no
#     Procuracio Reial (the king's revenue office) and no Curia de la
#     Governacio.
#   vanilla Mallorca elsewhere: the Leges Palatinae are a policy of the legal
#     code (laws/01_legal_system.txt), the Pearl of the Mediterranean a reform
#     (government_reforms/country_specific.txt:4611), Sa Llotja a monument
#     (building_types/unique_buildings.txt:4294), the Consolat de Mar a
#     privilege the country starts with, the lieutenancy an advance with a
#     building, the coast towers and the Carta de Franquesa tag advances.
#   the mod's Catalonia, Aragonese Empire, Aragon and Valencia (99_nd_cat.txt,
#     99_nd_cda.txt, 99_nd_ara.txt, 99_nd_val.txt): the Consell de Cent, the
#     Batllia General, the vegueries, the Tribunal de Contrafaccions, the
#     chancery, the Generalitat, the Consolat de Mar, the Sommaria, the Real
#     Audiencia, three Aragonese courts, the Mustassaf, the Pare d'Orfens, the
#     Lligallo and the Governacio. So no mostassaf, no veguer de fora, no
#     Audiencia of 1571, no governor.
#   this country's own tree (advances and building_types/99_nd_mll.txt): the
#     Sindicat de Fora and its house, the Contracte Sant and the Universal
#     Consignacio, the signal towers, the Estudi General, the Consolat de Mar
#     and the island governors of the destiny tree. None is rewritten here.
#
# What is left are four bodies of the Universitat de la Ciutat e Regne:
# 1. Gran i General Consell: the assembly of city and villages.
# 2. Juraria: the six jurats who govern city and kingdom.
# 3. Morberia: the health board that holds ships at Portopi.
# 4. Col.legi de la Mercaderia: the merchants' college that keeps the harbour.
#
# Estate alignment, on the main road (Mallorca itself, a monarchy with all
# four estates): villages and townsmen against the king's officers; the city
# against the villages; the island at large against the shipping interest;
# merchants against the knights their college shuts out.
#
# No government power key is granted: one road into the tag is a theocracy
# (Andorra).
#
# Slots. Vanilla gives Mallorca none on the day it forms (a capital grants one
# only as a megalopolis, location_ranks/00_default.txt:50). The auto modifier
# grants 2, half of these four. Vanilla adds one with the age 4 bureaucracy
# advance and one in age 6, and from Confessionalism its ten generic offices
# compete for the same slots.
#
# No cabinet seat and no character modifier. No key stands in both the funded
# and the neglected block of one institution. All IDs are mll_-prefixed.
#
# Sources, fetched 2026-10-02 through the ca.wikipedia API: "Gran i General
# Consell", "Jurats de la Ciutat i Regne de Mallorca", "Morberia", "Col.legi
# de la Mercaderia de Mallorca", "Institucions del Regne de Mallorca",
# "Procuracio Reial de Mallorca". What rests on memory is marked below.

# 1. Gran i General Consell
# Created with the jurats on 7 July 1249 and abolished on 22 July 1718. At
# first the jurats' advisers; by Sancho's arbitral sentence of 19 June 1315
# the villages got fixed seats and a voice over public spending, and the
# council's decisions bound the jurats. Mallorca never had Corts: this
# assembly voted the kingdom's own taxes, saw to the wheat supply and sent
# syndics abroad. After 1447 it had 84 members, 56 of the city and 28 of the
# Part Forana; 72 from 1614. For most of its life a decision needed two
# thirds of the members, "which made decisions difficult" (ca.wikipedia).
# The crown is the estate that dislikes it: the article describes kings and
# viceroys reserving the choice of councillors (1373) and vetoing names.
# Funded, the villages consent to the talles they have seen voted and
# accounted for. Unpaid, nobody comes, the quorum fails and nothing passes.

	implementation_price = price:implement_bureaucracy_price

}

# 2. Juraria
# Six jurats, licensed by James I on 7 July 1249: after 1382 a knight (the
# jurat en cap), two citizens, two merchants and one artisan, for one year,
# unpaid and unable to refuse. They sat in the Sala de la Juraria on the
# Placa de Cort. Their first duty was to defend the franchises against the
# king's officers; they ran the Universitat's money, the wheat supply, the
# defences and the hospitals. Chosen by co-optation, then by a run of royal
# pragmatics, and from 1447 to 1718 by lot from sacks of approved names
# (sort i sac), which lasted, the article says, because filling the sacks
# was cheaper than paying the king for a new regime.
# The villages are the estate that dislikes them: the city's magistrates
# governed the whole island, which is what the Revolta Forana of 1450 was
# about (the country's own advance nd_mll_revolta_forana).
# An office of the City of Mallorca: it needs Palma.
# Funded, wheat is bought in time and the city is held. Unpaid, the bandols
# fill the sacks, the taxes are farmed to friends and the villages stop
# obeying.
mll_juraria_bureaucracy = {
	implementation_price = price:implement_bureaucracy_price

	}

	potential = {

	}

	allow = {
		owns = location:palma

# 3. Morberia
# Created by the Gran i General Consell in 1467, with statutes from 1475: a
# standing commission of morbers without whose leave no ship could land
# people or goods. Crews under suspicion kept quarantine, first in the
# towers of Portopi; a lazaretto was agreed in 1497; a Junta Superior de
# Sanitat replaced the board in 1718 (ca.wikipedia, "Morberia"). The
# epidemics of 1475, 1481 and 1493 are what made it permanent
# (ca.wikipedia, "Historia de Mallorca").
# From memory, not fetched: that the physician Lluc Colomines wrote the
# statutes of 1475, and that shipmasters and merchants were the ones who
# resented the delays, which is why the burghers are the estate that
# dislikes it.
# The quarantine was kept in the towers of Portopi, so the funded side reads
# the country's own advance for those towers in the scale, as vanilla's
# Barid reads an advance (bureaucracies/muslim.txt). Sized for that case:
# 0.00009 with the towers, 0.00006 without.
# 0.003, a light institution: a commission of a few men and a tower.
# Funded, the sickness stays on the ships. Unpaid, the morbers sell their
# leave and hold whoever will not pay, and the harbour empties.
mll_morberia_bureaucracy = {

# 4. Col.legi de la Mercaderia
# Approved in principle by John I in 1394, with a first rule of 11 May 1403
# and a royal ordinance of Martin the Humane in 1409 that gave it a duty on
# goods in transit, the diner de la mercaderia. Two defenedors de la
# mercaderia, a clavari and a council of twenty merchants kept the mole of
# the city and the harbour of Portopi; the books of the diner del moll show
# the money going to harbour works and defensive towers. From 1454 its
# officers were drawn by sort i sac. Knights and donzells were shut out, and
# some gave up their rank to hold the office. It lost its taxes with the
# Nova Planta and lasted until 1800 (ca.wikipedia).
# It is not the Consolat de Mar (a court, and vanilla's privilege) and not
# Sa Llotja (its hall, and vanilla's monument): this is the body that taxed
# cargo to keep the port.
# From memory, not fetched: that the same dues armed ships against corsairs
# (the victigal del mar of its account books suggests it).
# The mole is Palma's: it needs the city.
# Funded, the mole is sound, the roads are watched and trade costs less to
# carry. Unpaid, the dues are farmed, the harbour silts and corsairs lie
# off the bay.
