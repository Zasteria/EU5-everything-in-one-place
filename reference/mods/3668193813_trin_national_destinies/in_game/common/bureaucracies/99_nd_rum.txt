# National Destinies: Sultanate of Rum bureaucracies

# Ottomans as the winner in the Ottomans' place (RUM_f, allow), so its
# institutions are those of the Seljuk sultans of Rum whose name it takes,
# not those of the Porte. The seven keys keep their old Ottoman names (saved
# games and the generated impact modifiers hold them); names and texts were
# re-themed on 2026-10-02 and every grant kept, since each Ottoman office
# here grew out of the Seljuk one that now stands in its place.
# Slots: see the auto modifier file.
# All history in this file is written from memory, not fetched.
