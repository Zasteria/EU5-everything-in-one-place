# ============================================================================
# Occitania (OCC): custom buildings
# National Destinies mod
# ============================================================================
#
# Every building below carries a location_potential and a comment saying why
# that reach is correct. Three of them are single institutions and are gated
# to one location by is_capital or its negation; the industrial ones are gated
# to the raw material they actually worked; the rest are gated by settlement
# tier and terrain.
#
# No building here depends on holding the Narbonne coastal corridor, which
# Regnum Gothorum (GOT) contests.
