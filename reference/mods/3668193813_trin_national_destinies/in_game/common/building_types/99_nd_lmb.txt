# Lombardy (LMB) custom buildings
# The Lombard plain as Europe's best-watered, best-armed, best-banked
# workshop: Monza's crown treasury, Cistercian water meadows, the
# Missaglia armouries, the Ca' Granda, the Visconti citadel, the Monte
# di Pieta, the Navigli, the rice estates, the Garda paper valley,
# Borromean seminaries, the Ambrosiana, the Lazzaretto, Cremona's
# luthier workshops, the lake villas, and Volta's physics cabinet.
