# === Outremer (OUT) Static Modifiers ===
# Level 4: 40 year duration on formation choices, max 8 modifiers per choice.
# Every choice here answers the same question, which is the country's own
# formation clause: Catholics are at most half the realm, so on what basis
# does a Latin emperor govern subjects he is forbidden to convert.

# ===== FORMATION CHOICES =====
