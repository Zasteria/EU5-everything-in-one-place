Local development adaptation of Child Education 1.0.1 by grackbox (Workshop 3815401472).
Original author credited; no claim of original authorship for the education algorithms or icons.

Enable this mod; disable the original Child Education (Workshop 3815401472) and the separate Auto Child Education (Workshop 3681510822). Community Mod Framework is not required by this mod; keep it only if another active mod needs it.
When using Glorp UI, load this mod BEFORE Glorp UI so Glorp's pause-menu templates win. The education button is attached to the pause-menu window separately from those templates.
Open a country, press Esc, select Auto Child Education (Otomatik Çocuk Eğitimi in Turkish). Settings persist in that country's save variables. General and paid education tabs retain all original settings. Defaults: alerts on, ruler children/grandchildren and first 3 successors with descendants, boys only, auto off, no paid traits.
Both tracking modes include the ruler's children and grandchildren, subject to the age, gender, court and future-royal filters. Monthly processing preserves manually selected education and the original paid price/affordability checks. The independent alert is above the right side of the screen. Refresh list only scans; it does not purchase or assign education.
Original character-panel right-click shortcut is not included; vanilla/Glorp character panels remain in control.
Do not enable alongside the original: the shared education IDs would collide. Existing framework settings are not automatically migrated. Multiplayer requires runtime validation.
Overrides vanilla ingame_menu.gui (1.4.4); other mods replacing that file need a compatibility merge. No installed game or Workshop source files were modified.
Version 0.2.2 runtime verification on EU5 1.4.4: loaded an existing Ottoman save, opened the settings and child panel, ran natural monthly pulses, and saved the result. All 9 previously excluded ruler daughters received education; 6 existing specialized educations were preserved. Paid education and multiplayer were not part of this regression test.
