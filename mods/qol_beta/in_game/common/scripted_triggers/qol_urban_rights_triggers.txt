# right's boosted goods. A second "strong" tier at 50% once drew a larger icon for a larger share;
# the two treatments proved indistinguishable in play, so the exact figure now reaches the player
# through the marker tooltip, where it was always the legible part, and the icon just means "matches".
#

# matches Masonry Rights at all.
